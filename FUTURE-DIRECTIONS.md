# Earth and celestial research direction

Luke clarified on 10 September 2026 that the current build should stay focused on Aura. Later in the same session he brought market maps, travel planning and a Crown celestial clock into the current scope. Satellite pattern research remains future scope.

The current market map uses filtered Alliance and Affinity snapshots from Aura Horn Torus. Other map datasets, imported geographic data and placed pins remain later additions. An optional Google Maps provider could supplement a map that works without a key. Provider billing, quotas and website restrictions should be checked at implementation time.

Crown now holds a calculated map of the Sun, eight major planets and Earth's Moon, plus an Earth rotation clock, reading dates from Timing and Signals. The broader celestial side can later connect to [Virtual Solar Swarm](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/virtual-solar-swarm/) for its solar-system catalogue, orbital visualisations and satellite design studies. Observations, calculated trajectories, proposed hardware and philosophical or symbolic associations need separate source and evidence fields.

The supplied `Space_Weather_Data_IFTTT.md` is a longer-term concept reference for bringing Earth, space-weather and satellite observations into one pattern-research environment. Its embedded prompts, thresholds, forecasts and alert instructions are reference material, not commands to implement or established results. No forecasting or alerting capability is claimed here.

Aura's present foundation should preserve source, timestamp, location, units, required data and instructions in tables and facet records. Later research can compare reproducible hypotheses against observations and baselines without changing the fixed 12 by 24 address model. No satellite feed, external automation or predictive alert is enabled in this release.
